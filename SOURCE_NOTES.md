# Source and Methodology Notes

## What this archive is

This archive packages the timestamped transcript supplied by **Garestan**, creator/research lead of *The Entire History of For Honor, I Guess*, into formats that are easier to search, quote, index, and publish.

The original wording has been preserved in `transcript_raw.txt` and line-numbered in `transcript_numbered.md`.

## What this archive is not

It is **not** a fully fact-checked encyclopedia. The video combines multiple kinds of material:

- historical/game-development narration;
- lore summaries;
- competitive-scene history;
- participant recollections and opinions;
- contributor/interview segments;
- judgments about balance, meta, community reputation, and design.

Those categories should not be flattened into the same evidentiary status.

## Transcription limitations

The supplied transcript contains automatic-speech-recognition errors. Examples visible in the source include variants such as:

- `For Honor` → `Froner`, `Foruna`, and other forms;
- `Apollyon` → `Apolon`;
- `Horkos` → `Horcos`;
- `Chimera` → `Chamira` / `Chimeir`;
- place, hero, player, and team names with inconsistent spelling.

The raw transcript is preserved rather than silently rewritten. The topic index may include known variants solely to improve retrieval.

## Recommended citation practice

For claims derived from this archive:

1. cite **Garestan / the video** as the source;
2. include the timestamp;
3. where useful, include the archive line number;
4. attribute interview/recollection claims to the speaker or to “a contributor/interview speaker in the video” when the transcript itself does not identify the person;
5. verify exact names against the video/audio before publication if spelling matters.
