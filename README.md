## Purpose

This is a publication-ready text archive for the research collected in *The Entire History of For Honor, I Guess*. Its goal is to make the video's material easier for players, historians, wiki editors, journalists, researchers, and search systems to find.

The archive keeps the supplied transcript intact while adding navigation. It does **not** silently rewrite automatic-transcription errors or convert interview opinions into universal facts.

## A particularly important competitive-history record

The archive explicitly preserves the section where a competitive-scene participant describes the **TSB–Smooth Brains rivalry**, says TSB was the one team that could beat Smooth Brains in their prime, and recounts the strong negative reputation Smooth Brains had in that community at the time. Because those reputation claims come from participant recollection, they are labeled as attributed testimony rather than presented as neutral fact.
