## Use with care

- Statements about a team's reputation, toxicity, rivalries, or who was considered 'best' are often recollections from participants and should be attributed to the speaker/video when quoted elsewhere.
- The supplied transcript does not consistently identify speakers. The `>>` markers are preserved where present, but they do not provide a complete speaker map.
- Proper nouns are frequently misrecognized by automatic transcription. Consult the video/audio when exact spelling matters.
